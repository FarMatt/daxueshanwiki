Plan your first visit to Daxueshan

A quick guide to reaching the forest recreation area, checking current details, and planning safely.

Where is it?

Daxueshan National Forest Recreation Area is in Heping District, Taichung. The destination sits above 2,000 metres; Tianchi is listed at 2,600 metres.

What to plan first

Choose your transport, confirm the date-specific return journey, check access notices, and leave time for mountain-road conditions.

This is a learning summary from five source records, not a live booking or dispatch service.

Sources: Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency, park overview (S02); Taichung Tourism, Nature and Ecology (S05).
Getting there · 2 of 8

Choose a route from Taichung or Fengyuan

From Taichung HSR

The park overview describes bus 153 to Dongshi, then bus 252 to the recreation area. It lists bus 252 as a Saturday and Sunday service.

From Fengyuan

The park overview lists Taiwan Tourist Shuttle route 889, or local buses 90, 206, or 207 to Dongshi followed by bus 252.

Use the live operator timetable for your travel date. Do not assume a service runs every day.

Sources: Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency, park overview (S02); Taiwan Forest Recreation, trails (S03).

Trails and nature · 5 of 8

Expect changing forest and mountain weather

Forest zones

The official trail page describes changes from broadleaf forest to mixed forest and conifer forest as elevation changes.

Weather and seasons

The area is in a cloud-forest belt with changeable weather. The trail page describes seasonal flowers and autumn/winter leaf colour.

Seasonal descriptions are not a forecast. Check live weather, trail status, and closures before setting out.

Sources: Taiwan Forest Recreation, trails (S03); Taichung Tourism, Nature and Ecology (S05).
Road, rules and facilities · 6 of 8

Prepare for a mountain road and park rules

Road and vehicle checks

The park warns of fog, slippery roads after rain, rockfall, and fallen trees. It also lists vehicle restrictions on the forest road; check the current notice before driving or arranging a tour.

Facilities and contact

The park page lists a visitor center, parking areas, trails, and accommodation. It gives the address as No. 18, Xueshan Road, Heping District, Taichung City, and service phone +886-4-2587-7901.

The park page prohibits bringing dogs, cats, and other mammals. Verify current rules and facility availability directly with the park.

Source: Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency, park overview (S02).
Trip checklist · 7 of 8

Before leaving, run these five checks

  1. Confirm the park is open and review road or trail notices.
  2. Check the correct bus timetable for your date and confirm reservation rules.
  3. Plan the return trip before starting the day; note the last available service.
  4. If driving, check vehicle restrictions and fuel needs before entering the mountain road.
  5. Recheck fees, facilities, weather, and park rules with official sources.

If a source is unclear or two official pages differ, contact the responsible operator instead of guessing.

Sources: Park overview (S02); 889 timetable (S04); Trail information (S03).

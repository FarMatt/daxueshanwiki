## Start here
Daxueshan National Forest Recreation Area is a mountain destination in Heping District, Taichung. Use the official sources below for current visitor planning. This Wiki is a learning summary, not a live travel service.

## Getting there
The official park overview describes routes from Taichung HSR via bus 153 to Dongshi and weekend bus 252, and from Fengyuan via Taiwan Tourist Shuttle 889 or local buses to Dongshi then bus 252. The official shuttle timetable, when accessed on 2026-11-22, showed departures from Fengyuan at 07:15, 07:30, 13:30, and 13:45. Check the live timetable and seat-reservation rules before leaving; the park page and route page describe reservation scope differently. [S02](https://recreation.forest.gov.tw/EN/Forest/RA?typ_id=0300003) [S04](https://www.taiwantrip.com.tw/Frontend/Bustime/TimeTable/R0110) [S03](https://recreation.forest.gov.tw/EN/Trail/RT?tr_id=049)

## Hours, entry, and parking
The official park page lists park hours as 06:30–17:00 and visitor-center hours as 07:30–17:30. It lists holiday full admission at NT$200 and parking at NT$100 for large/small vehicles and NT$20 for motorcycles (heavy motorcycles NT$100). Eligibility-based discounts and free admission also appear on the official page. These figures may change; verify the live official page before travel. [S02](https://recreation.forest.gov.tw/EN/Forest/RA?typ_id=0300003)

## Trails and nature
The official trail page describes forest zones that change with altitude, cloud-forest weather, and seasonal scenery. The Taichung tourism page gives broader context on the area's elevation zones, forest species, and logging history. The Taiwan Outdoors guide describes hiking options and accommodations, but its travel advice is secondary and some details may be old. [S03](https://recreation.forest.gov.tw/EN/Trail/RT?tr_id=049) [S05](https://www.taichung.travel/en/experience/natural) [S01](https://taiwanoutdoors.com/hiking/national-forests/daxueshan-national-forest/)

## Safety and facilities
The official park page warns about rugged, foggy mountain roads, slippery conditions after rain, falling rocks, and fallen trees. It gives the recreation area's address and service phone. Confirm road access and facility availability with the agency before departure. [S02](https://recreation.forest.gov.tw/EN/Forest/RA?typ_id=0300003)

## Quick planning checklist
1. Check the official park hours, access notices, and admission details.
2. Check the date-specific shuttle or bus timetable; reserve the required mountain segment in advance.
3. Confirm the last return trip and leave enough time for mountain travel.
4. Check weather and trail status with official sources.
5. Recheck current details on the day of travel.
